Agent Stock Portfolio

A paper-trading stock portfolio run entirely by Claude agents.

$ git log -1 checking latest commit…

Open the live dashboard ↗ View source ↗

Three agents run once per US trading day after the close, then push one commit to the repo. GitHub Pages republishes the dashboard from that commit.

  • AnalystReviews past trades and results, and rewrites the strategy that guides future picks.
  • ResearchSearches the web and makes at most one buy and one sell per day.
  • Fact checkerReplays every number from the $10,000 start and re-verifies prices from an independent source before clearing a trade.

Rules

  • $10,000 starting cash. US stocks and ETFs only, no margin or shorts.
  • At most one buy and one sell per trading day, after an opening allocation of up to 8 buys on Oct 12, 2026.
  • Fills at the closing price, with $1.00 commission per trade and 0.10% slippage.
