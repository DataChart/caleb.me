My time at Windsor FTC Robotics

From January to July 2019, I worked as an Associate Engineer and Teacher with Windsor FTC Robotics, through STEM Education Inc.

Teaching

A big part of the job was teaching. I taught programming, robotics and engineering concepts to high school classes in rural Kenya.

Engineering

The engineering side was a solar installation project that I co-led through its full lifecycle, from planning to delivering light to 24 rural homes. I wrote that project up on its own in Bringing solar light to 24 homes in rural Kenya.
