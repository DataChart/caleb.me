Work

My time as an applied data scientist and professional poker player

From August 2025 to April 2026 I worked independently as an applied data scientist and professional poker player, on-site in Blackhawk, Colorado. Alongside playing, I built a research setup to study the game the way I'd study any decision problem: with simulation, reinforcement learning and careful evaluation.

The environment

I built a custom reinforcement-learning environment in Python on the OpenAI Gym interface that simulates an agent playing 8-player No-Limit Texas Hold'em. Defining it meant pinning down the game-state observations, the legal action space, the reward function, opponent behavior, betting transitions and terminal conditions.

It's the same kind of work as my Minesweeper agent, just with hidden information and seven opponents.

Simulation and solvers

On top of the environment I engineered a game-theoretic simulation pipeline that ran 10 million simulated games and generated more than 150 million records, 22 GB in total. I benchmarked tabular counterfactual regret minimization (CFR) against Deep CFR, and trained a PPO actor-critic agent to learn decision policies and approximate equilibrium strategies.

Measuring it honestly

I evaluated policies with bb/100 win rates and 5 other performance metrics, using bootstrap-derived 95% confidence intervals. The optimized policies generated 5 times the cash returns of a naive baseline, and the deep-learning approach beat tabular CFR by 40% on bb/100.

Finally, I used KPIs from the simulations to train an XGBoost model that forecasts long-term returns. On an external validation dataset it reached 95% forecast accuracy, measured as 1 minus MAPE.

At a glance

  • Role: Applied Data Scientist and Professional Poker Player, independent
  • Where: Blackhawk, Colorado
  • When: August 2025 to April 2026
  • Methods: Custom OpenAI Gym environment, tabular CFR, Deep CFR, PPO, XGBoost, bootstrap confidence intervals
  • Results: 10M simulated games (22 GB); 5x a naive baseline; Deep beat tabular CFR by 40% on bb/100; 95% forecast accuracy
