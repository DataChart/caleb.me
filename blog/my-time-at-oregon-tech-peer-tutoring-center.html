My time at the Oregon Tech Peer Tutoring Center

From February 2022 to June 2025, alongside my data science degree, I worked as a Public Speaking Tutor at the Peer Tutoring Center at Oregon Institute of Technology.

What the job was

Each semester I supported up to four classes. Students came to me for three kinds of help: coaching on how they delivered a speech, help drafting the speech itself, and help with speaking anxiety.

How it went

I kept a 100% satisfaction rate across student feedback and formal evaluations for the whole time I was there.

What I took from it

I've written more about teaching and leading a room in facilitation skills for leaders.

At a glance

  • Role: Public Speaking Tutor
  • Where: Peer Tutoring Center, Oregon Institute of Technology
  • When: February 2022 to June 2025
  • Scope: Up to 4 classes per semester
  • Results: 100% satisfaction rate in student feedback and formal evaluations
