R & Dashboards

A writing tracker in R Shiny: 151,170 words in six months

Source on GitHub

github.com/DataChart/WritingTrackerApp

Outside of data science, I write science fiction. I've written two novels, and the hardest part was never the ideas, it was showing up every day. So I did what a data scientist does: I logged it, and then I built a dashboard for it.

The data

Every day went into a simple spreadsheet with the date, a daily word count, and how those words split across three kinds of writing: personal writing, outlines and ideas, and the official story. Each day had a goal of 1,000 words. A second sheet rolled everything up by month with a monthly goal.

From January to June 2024 that log covers 150 days and 151,170 words.

January was the only month that beat its goal (22,006 words against 20,000). After that I kept raising the bar faster than I could clear it, which is either ambition or a bug in my goal-setting. The best month was April, with 36,311 words, and nearly 17,000 of them went into the official story.

The app

The dashboard is an R Shiny app with four tabs:

  • Day: a radar chart (fmsb) comparing today against my all-time average and the last 30 days across all three kinds of writing, plus a small summary table.
  • Month: pick a month and a category and get an interactive plotly bar chart of every day, next to the full data table.
  • Year: a grouped bar chart of word count by category for every month, so you can see where the words actually went.
  • Input: a placeholder for logging words straight from the app, which is still on the to-do list.

Under the hood it's tidyverse for the reshaping (a pivot_longer turns the monthly sheet into one row per category), ggplot2 wrapped in plotly for the interactive charts, DT for the tables, and a sandstone theme from shinythemes.
