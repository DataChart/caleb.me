My time at Poudre Valley REA

In May 2026 I joined Poudre Valley REA as an AI & BI Specialist I, on a three-person AI and BI team. The job sits right where my interests meet: data engineering, automation, dashboards and applied AI, plus a lot of teaching.

Data governance and DataOps

I co-lead enterprise data governance for the team. That means setting standards and auditing everything we own (databases, ETL pipelines, reports, automations, scripts and AI assets) for utilization, performance, lineage and lifecycle management.

I also brought Git and GitHub source control to hundreds of analytics assets and set the DataOps standards around it. In my first six months I contributed more than 100 commits, averaging 32 a week. I wrote 6 operational procedures the department now uses routinely, covering data quality, documentation, source control, lifecycle management and AI readiness.

Automation and integrations

I architected and delivered 8 end-to-end automation initiatives, from requirements gathering through development, validation and deployment. Together they eliminate more than 200 hours of manual work a year. Four of them are API-driven data integrations that combine ETL, authentication, data modeling and cybersecurity controls to fully automate what used to be manual data entry. Along the way I wrote 13 automation scripts in Python, PowerShell and C# for ETL, APIs, data processing and system automation.

The data underneath is large: multiple datasets over 100 GB and relational environments with more than 500 tables, which I work across for transformation, reporting and performance optimization.

Dashboards and the cloud

I've delivered more than 35 customized dashboards for business users: over 20 Power BI reports, 11 HTML dashboards and 4 Azure-hosted applications. I also led the move to the cloud for real-time dashboards, Azure applications, machine-learning forecasting and secure cloud data infrastructure.

AI at work

On the AI side, I develop and maintain RAG systems for enterprise document retrieval and LLM-enabled workflows using OneDrive, SharePoint and Microsoft Copilot. I also led AI training for 60 employees across 9 departments, building role-specific workflows for each group, and effective AI usage went up 300%.

At a glance

  • Role: AI & BI Specialist I, full-time
  • Where: Poudre Valley REA
  • When: May 2026 to present
  • Tools: Power BI, SQL, Python, PowerShell, C#, Azure, Git and GitHub, Microsoft Copilot
  • Results: 8 automations saving 200+ hours a year; 35+ dashboards; AI training for 60 employees; effective AI usage up 300%
