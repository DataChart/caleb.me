Engineering & Teaching

Bringing solar light to 24 homes in rural Kenya

From January to July 2019, I worked as an Associate Engineer and Teacher with Windsor FTC Robotics through STEM Education Inc. It's a very different kind of project from the rest of this blog, with no data science involved.

The solar project

I co-led the full lifecycle of a solar installation project, from start to finish, and together we delivered light to 24 rural homes in Kenya.

Teaching

Alongside the installation, I taught programming, robotics and engineering concepts to high school classes in rural Kenya. Teaching something forces you to understand it properly.

At a glance

  • Role: Associate Engineer and Teacher, Windsor FTC Robotics (STEM Education Inc.)
  • When: January to July 2019
  • Where: Rural Kenya
  • Results: Solar light delivered to 24 rural homes; taught programming, robotics and engineering to high school classes
