Statistics

What 131 variables of RuneScape hiscores say about players

For Statistical Machine Learning I (STAT441, Spring 2024), I got to combine two things I love: statistics and video games. The dataset was RuneScape hiscores, the public leaderboards that track every player's levels and experience across the game's skills and activities. Every player became a row with 131 variables.

Predicting player trends

I compared many statistical models to predict trends in how players progress. With 131 variables, the hard part is less about fitting a model and more about picking one that generalizes, and comparing several approaches side by side made that choice a lot clearer.

Clustering for storytelling

I also ran an unsupervised clustering analysis with K-means. Clustering doesn't need labels: it groups players who look alike across all those variables. The result was great for visualization and storytelling, because each cluster becomes a kind of player you can describe.

Keeping it fast

A dataset that wide can get slow quickly, so I made a lot of optimizations to keep computation time low and got training times under two hours.

At a glance

  • Course: STAT441 Statistical Machine Learning I, Oregon Institute of Technology
  • When: Spring 2024
  • Methods: Comparison of statistical models, K-means clustering
  • Results: Modeled player trends across 131 variables, with training under 2 hours
